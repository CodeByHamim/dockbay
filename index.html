FAQ

Honest answers, current state

What is Dockbay?
A developer hosting platform in the making: connect a GitHub repo, push code, and your app runs with HTTPS, logs, and rollbacks — at a fixed monthly price with a hard spending cap. It's currently in the research phase, not a finished product.
When does it launch?
We don't have a launch date yet, and we'd rather not promise one. Joining the waitlist is the best way to hear first when there's something to try.
What happens when I hit my spend cap?
The plan is simple: your apps pause or throttle instead of ever charging you more than the cap you set. You get a warning before it happens and can raise the cap yourself. The exact mechanics are still being designed.
What can I deploy?
The goal is to support common web apps and APIs — anything you'd deploy on a similar platform today. The supported runtimes and build options aren't finalized yet.
Is there a free tier?
Not decided yet. We're testing interest in low fixed prices first. Tell us what you need in the form — it genuinely shapes the plan.
Where will servers be located?
The first region is still being decided. EU-based hosting is being considered, but nothing is confirmed — we won't claim a location until it's real.
Can I migrate from Render, Heroku or Railway?
That's the plan — if you're deploying from a Git repo today, migration should be a repo connection and a config file away. Migration tooling details will come closer to launch.
